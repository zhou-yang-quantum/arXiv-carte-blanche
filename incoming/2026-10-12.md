### Exact solutions of non-Markovian dissipative Ising models
**Mohammad Maghrebi — arXiv:2610.12546**

Maghrebi identifies an exactly solvable class of interacting spin systems coupled to lossy bosonic modes. These modes mediate Ising interactions while retaining memory, and the spins can simultaneously undergo Markovian dephasing, relaxation, pumping, or even dissipative processes that generate coherences. An exact stochastic representation reduces the many-body calculation to individual-spin evolution, giving spin observables and correlations without adiabatically eliminating the bosons.

The construction excludes a transverse field: its solvability does not extend to a generic driven Ising Hamiltonian. Within its scope, however, local dissipation need not commute with the interactions. The Markovian limit includes correlated dephasing and nonreciprocal interactions. A spin-squeezing example shows that environmental memory and local dissipation jointly influence both attainable squeezing and the best preparation time.

**Background.** Eliminating a mediator assumes that it responds sufficiently quickly to the spins. Resonators and vibrational modes can violate this assumption, making an apparently simple spin model acquire memory. Exact treatments are particularly valuable when interactions and noise prevent ordinary few-body methods from closing.

**Why it matters for you:** This directly joins non-equilibrium physics, exact solvability, and quantum simulation. It provides a controlled benchmark for deciding when a reduced Markovian model captures useful dynamics and when the mediator must remain explicit.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 2

### An Instability of the Quantum Markov Length
**David C. Newsom, Michael Levin — arXiv:2610.12929**

The authors construct arbitrarily weak local channels that turn a product vacuum into a state with infinite quantum Markov length. Their fermionic example uses only two layers of nearest-neighbor Gaussian channels, yet conditional mutual information decays algebraically with buffer width. They also construct a qubit analogue and establish instability for translation-invariant states possessing a rank-deficient reduced density operator.

The mechanism is the nonanalyticity of von Neumann entropy near zero eigenvalues. Crucially, the explicit example requires fine-tuning: changing the channel's relative coupling weights or giving the initial product state a finite temperature restores exponential decay. The result therefore defeats a general stability claim without establishing that generic weak noise produces the same singular behavior.

**Background.** Conditional mutual information measures correlations between separated regions that remain after conditioning on the intervening buffer. In entropy notation, $I(A:C|B)=S(AB)+S(BC)-S(B)-S(ABC)$. The Markov length characterizes its exponential decay and has been used to formulate local reversibility and mixed-state phase equivalence.

**Why it matters for you:** This challenges an information-theoretic diagnostic used in noisy many-body physics. It highlights how finite-depth local dynamics can produce singular entropy behavior even when ordinary correlation data remain short-ranged, a useful warning when connecting noise, recovery, and phase classification.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 3

### Error correction and quantum information spreading on sparse random graphs
**Giovanni Cemin, Marin Bukov, Andrea Solfanelli — arXiv:2610.12597**

Using Floquet random Clifford circuits on two sparse graph architectures, the authors connect information spreading, an entanglement transition, and quantum-code distance through the spectral dimension $d_s$. This dimension is extracted from the low-energy graph-Laplacian spectrum rather than from an embedding in ordinary space. Their circuits exhibit a transition between localized area-law and ergodic volume-law entanglement, with long-range percolation criticality organized by spectral dimension.

In the ergodic phase, the circuit supplies a Calderbank–Shor–Steane code encoder. Its distance reflects competing logical sectors associated with cutting the graph and with its shortest winding cycle. Increasing connectivity affects these vulnerabilities in opposite directions, producing an optimum at intermediate spectral dimension. The evidence concerns the studied architectures and Clifford circuit ensemble; sparse connectivity alone does not establish a practical fault-tolerant implementation or circuit-noise threshold.

**Background.** Higher-dimensional connectivity can improve coding properties while making hardware connections harder to realize. Sparse graphs offer intermediate geometries whose diffusion and propagation properties need not resemble a regular lattice. Code distance measures the smallest undetectable logical error.

**Why it matters for you:** This makes quantum error correction a problem in non-equilibrium statistical mechanics and geometry. It offers a physical organizing principle for encoder design and illustrates why adding connections need not improve every logical sector simultaneously.

### The Quasiparticle Ansatz for Relativistic Continuous Matrix Product States
**Silviu S. Pufu, Benjamin T. Søgaard, Spyros Terlemes — arXiv:2610.13824**

This work extends relativistic continuous matrix product states from vacuum calculations to low-lying particles and solitons. Excitation energies follow from an effective Hamiltonian eigenproblem. The construction works directly in the continuum and infinite volume, including solitons that interpolate between distinct degenerate vacua.

The authors benchmark sine-Gordon soliton and breather masses against integrability, obtaining good agreement. They then study $\phi^4$ theory across its symmetry-preserving and symmetry-broken phases, mapping when stable particles and solitons exist. Deep in the broken phase they resolve two stable particles alongside the soliton sector. The higher particle's numerical data are not fully converged in bond dimension, so the variational framework is more firmly established than uniform numerical precision throughout the spectrum.

**Background.** Matrix product states compress many-body wavefunctions through a finite auxiliary dimension. Their relativistic continuum counterparts seek the same computational advantages without a spatial lattice. Solitons connect different vacua, while sine-Gordon breathers are bound states of soliton–antisoliton pairs.

**Why it matters for you:** This is a useful quantum-field-theory algorithm with direct physical outputs: masses, stability thresholds, and topological excitations. Exact sine-Gordon results provide a benchmark before applying the method to a nonintegrable theory, linking solvable models to continuum numerical simulation.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 5

### Locality-Preparation Time Tradeoffs in Dissipative Quantum Systems
**Philipp Westhoff, Sara Murciano, Mattia Moroder — arXiv:2610.13784**

The authors derive an exact relation between dissipator locality and relaxation time for quadratic fermionic systems. They exploit freedom to mix quasiparticle annihilation operators while preserving their common vacuum, thereby improving the spatial localization of the jump operators without changing the target steady state.

For the critical Kitaev chain, suppressing jump-operator tails to $r^{-(n+1)}$ entails an asymptotic relaxation timescale $\tau\sim N^{2n}/\kappa$, with chain size N and overall dissipation scale kappa. Both trivial and topological gapped phases instead permit exponentially localized jump operators with a finite relaxation timescale in the thermodynamic limit. Along a special curve, the operators become strictly finite-range.

Here preparation time is characterized through the slowest physically accessible asymptotic decay rate. A size-independent relaxation scale should therefore not automatically be read as a size-independent time to reach any specified global fidelity. The exact results also concern quadratic systems and the analyzed dissipative constructions.

**Background.** Reservoir engineering makes the desired state attractive under environmental coupling. The jump operators encode those couplings; their spatial extent determines the required control and connectivity. Critical states pose a special challenge because their correlations extend over arbitrarily long distances.

**Why it matters for you:** This quantitatively connects non-equilibrium relaxation to quantum-simulation resources. It separates the cost of criticality from topology in a controlled model and explains why improving locality can slow preparation even while preserving the target state exactly.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 6

### Replica Transition at Measurement-Altered Ising Criticality
**Kabir Khanna, Sara Murciano, Romain Vasseur — arXiv:2610.13802**

The replicated ensemble associated with measurements of the critical Ising order parameter has a phase transition precisely at $Q\to1$, the replica limit needed for physical Born-probability averages. Consequently, one cannot obtain the physical answer by smoothly continuing a calculation from either neighboring replica phase.

Boundary conformal field theory identifies the mechanism: an operator that changes boundary conditions becomes marginal at the physical replica point. This allows a direct perturbative analytic calculation of the universal constant contribution to the measurement record's Shannon entropy, previously known numerically. The same framework addresses measurement-averaged spin correlations near the projective fixed point. The entropy calculation is perturbative, rather than an exact closed-form determination of every post-measurement observable.

**Background.** Replica methods introduce copies of a system to calculate nonlinear averages. Analytic continuation then recovers the desired observable. Measurements enter the ground-state path integral as a defect that can be treated as a boundary after folding. A transition in replica number can obstruct the continuation even when the underlying unmeasured theory is well understood.

**Why it matters for you:** This connects field theory, measurement backaction, and non-equilibrium ensembles. It exposes a concrete failure mode of a widely used method and shows how boundary physics can recover universal information stored in classical measurement outcomes.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 7

### Dissipative Majorana Chain---Anomalies and Non-Invertible Symmetries
**Hung-Hsuan Teh, Tokiro Numasawa — arXiv:2610.12607**

Teh and Numasawa analyze symmetry algebras of Majorana chains under Lindblad dynamics by mapping the density operator to a state in a doubled Hilbert space. Ket and bra sectors carry separate strong symmetries, while diagonal combinations furnish weak symmetries. Comparing sectors with and without fermion-parity defects reveals the lattice precursor of the closed chain's modulo-eight anomaly, together with projective symmetry relations intrinsic to the open-system setting.

The authors match these lattice algebras to a doubled Majorana conformal field theory and develop lattice bosonization. For even chains with collective energy dephasing, combining defect sectors and applying fermion-parity projections produces an Ising Lindbladian with non-invertible Kramers–Wannier symmetries. Odd chains instead yield an Ising description with a duality defect. These are explicit constructions under specified dissipation and sector choices, rather than a classification of arbitrary noisy chains.

**Background.** An anomaly is an obstruction encoded in the way symmetries act, constraining possible low-energy realizations. Non-invertible symmetries generalize ordinary group transformations: their composition need not possess an inverse. Kramers–Wannier duality exchanges Ising order and disorder descriptions.

**Why it matters for you:** This gives a concrete bridge between quantum-field-theory symmetry ideas and open many-body dynamics. The doubled formulation clarifies which symmetry structures survive dissipation and which new distinctions emerge because density matrices have both ket and bra sectors.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 8

### Lattice Monte Carlo of $λφ^4$ on Euclidean de Sitter
**Angelo Caravano — arXiv:2610.12547**

Caravano performs nonperturbative Monte Carlo calculations of a massless quartic scalar theory on Euclidean de Sitter space, the four-sphere $S^4$. The regulator truncates complete spherical-harmonic multiplets, preserving the spacetime's isometries at finite cutoff instead of approximating them with a spatial mesh.

At weak coupling, the measured zero-mode distribution reproduces Starobinsky–Yokoyama stochastic equilibrium, with deviations tracking one-loop coupling renormalization. At strong bare coupling, the renormalized quartic coupling approaches a cutoff-dependent ceiling consistent with the renormalization-group screening picture. This supplies a nonperturbative test of infrared resummation and a de Sitter analogue of finite-cutoff triviality bounds.

The reached cutoffs do not establish the continuum limit. The paper explicitly leaves higher-cutoff studies and the infinite-bare-coupling endpoint open. Its Euclidean equilibrium calculation also does not directly simulate Lorentzian real-time evolution.

**Background.** Light scalar fields in de Sitter develop infrared contributions that undermine naive perturbation theory. Stochastic methods resum their long-wavelength behavior. A symmetry-preserving spectral regulator provides an independent numerical route to testing that description while retaining ultraviolet cutoff control.

**Why it matters for you:** This broadens the field-theory selection toward curved spacetime through a concrete computational advance. It links infrared stochastic physics, ultraviolet renormalization, and Monte Carlo methods without relying on elaborate string-theory machinery.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 9

### Fast computational primitives for La-cross qLDPC codes
**Andrea Di Fini, Tom Hartweg, Laura Pecorari, Guido Pupillo, Samuel Crew — arXiv:2610.12685**

This paper develops logical operations for La-cross hypergraph-product codes, addressing the gap between storing encoded qubits and computing with them. For a specified subclass, classical seed-code automorphisms and fold-transversal gates generate the full logical Clifford group up to Pauli corrections. An addressable surface-code interface supplies externally prepared resource states to selected logical qubits.

In a primitive complete-period family, rank-one logical operators possess as many pairwise disjoint physical representatives as the code distance. Those representatives enable parallel gauging and a space–time tradeoff in logical measurement. The paper gives examples beyond this family but also a counterexample showing that the disjoint-representative property does not hold for every La-cross code.

These codes are not asymptotically constant-rate; their attraction is finite-size performance and low connectivity. The work establishes computational constructions, rather than a complete hardware demonstration or an end-to-end resource comparison.

**Background.** Quantum low-density parity-check codes use checks involving relatively few qubits. Favorable memory properties do not automatically imply efficient logical gates. Clifford operations require an additional non-Clifford resource for universal computation, commonly delivered through injected magic states.

**Why it matters for you:** This addresses error correction at the level of usable encoded dynamics. Its physical emphasis on interfaces, measurement parallelism, and connectivity provides a useful complement to the geometry-and-distance perspective of Item 3.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 10

### An entangling Hubbard quasiparticle collider on a digital quantum processor
**Chan Bin Bark, Hanbyul Kim, Yoonil Koo, SeungBeom Hong, Seik Pak, Kwang Jun Ahn, Sangmo Cheon, Youngseok Kim, Moon Jip Park — arXiv:2610.13946**

The authors collide a doublon and a holon in a 21-site half-filled Hubbard chain encoded on 42 qubits of the ibm_boston processor. The measured transmission probability agrees with the Bethe-ansatz scattering prediction without fitted parameters. A doublon–doublon control collision lacks the characteristic interaction dependence and particle-number fluctuations seen for the distinct quasiparticle pair.

The transmitted and reflected branches generate the number-entanglement structure expected from unitary scattering. Site-resolved occupations reveal their relative populations and the corresponding particle-number fluctuations. However, the paper explicitly states that these measurements do not directly confirm coherence between the branches. Its coherent-superposition interpretation relies on the unitary model and agreement with scattering theory; an interference experiment would provide a more direct test.

**Background.** A doublon is a doubly occupied site and a holon an empty site relative to the half-filled background. The one-dimensional Hubbard model is integrable, giving an exact scattering matrix against which a programmable collision can be compared.

**Why it matters for you:** This experiment merits inclusion because it tests an important theoretical principle: exact quasiparticle scattering can be accessed through controlled quantum simulation. It also illustrates the distinction between measuring branch probabilities and verifying their coherence, which matters when interpreting entanglement claims on noisy hardware.

### Critical Ising model as the $M(4,3)$ minimal model of conformal field theory
**Dmitry Chelkak, Konstantin Izyurov, Kalle Kytölä — arXiv:2610.13705**

The authors construct a linear map from local spin polynomials of the critical lattice Ising model to the state space of its minimal-model conformal field theory. The kernel consists exactly of null fields—lattice expressions that vanish in every correlation. Every non-null polynomial receives a scaling dimension from its continuum image, and the appropriately rescaled field has a nontrivial correlation-function limit as the lattice mesh vanishes.

The limiting correlations are expressed through primary fields and the continuum Virasoro action. Constants, individual spins, and edge energies map to the expected identity, spin, and energy highest-weight states. The construction applies to arbitrary isoradial graphs. It rigorously establishes the operator correspondence for this model and graph class; it does not establish a corresponding theorem for generic lattice critical points.

**Background.** The Ising critical theory has central charge $c=1/2$. Primary fields and their Virasoro descendants organize its local operators. Isoradial embeddings place graph faces on circles of a common radius, supporting the discrete analytic structures used in the proof.

**Why it matters for you:** This substantially strengthens the microscopic foundation of a canonical exactly solvable field theory. It explains how continuum operator content emerges from lattice observables, providing a broader connection between critical statistical mechanics, conformal symmetry, and the interpretation of numerical lattice measurements.

## arXiv-2026-10-12 — Item 12

### The Real EFThedron: A Moment Geometry for the Nonperturbative S-Matrix
**Laurentiu Rodina, Anna Tokareva, Yongjun Xu — arXiv:2610.12615**

The authors extend the geometric formulation of effective-field-theory positivity bounds to full nonlinear unitarity. Fractional energy powers allow the amplitude's real part, alongside its imaginary part, to enter a bounded complex moment problem. Analyticity becomes linear consistency conditions requiring different integral kernels to reconstruct the same low-energy couplings.

In two dimensions, this framework determines exact allowed coupling regions whose boundary amplitudes are products of Castillejo–Dalitz–Dyson factors. In four dimensions, it supplies closed-form and semi-analytic bounds for a massless model and massive scalar scattering. For massive scalars, additional analyticity constraints tighten previously published dual bounds toward the primal region. The massless application neglects light-particle loops, and the four-dimensional results remain bounds rather than a complete solution of the space of quantum field theories.

**Background.** A moment problem asks whether proposed integral data arise from an admissible measure. Dispersion relations connect low-energy coefficients to scattering data, while unitarity limits allowed amplitudes and crossing relates scattering channels. The EFThedron expresses these consistency restrictions geometrically.

**Why it matters for you:** This adds a nonperturbative field-theory perspective independent of a chosen microscopic Hamiltonian. Its exact two-dimensional results connect naturally to solvable scattering theories, while its four-dimensional applications show how stronger consistency conditions sharpen physically allowed interactions.
